HABU — Deep-Ocean Exosuit

A product launch page for a deep-ocean exosuit — a AAA game intro crossed with Apple product storytelling.

RoleDesign & Engineering — solo
TimelineJuly 2026
FocusProduct Storytelling · Next.js · GSAP
SourceGitHub ↗

Context

A cinematic single-scroll landing page for a fictional deep-ocean exosuit — fully video-driven, with an interactive product-inspection section.

The Problem

Product pages list features. This one had to make you feel the object before explaining it — the spec sheet arrives after the emotion.

Process

01

A scene-stack architecture

Sticky scenes stack into film, so phase transitions flow instead of cutting.

02

Inspection as the product moment

A camera rig and spec panels let the visitor move around the object at their own pace.

03

Scroll-driven playback, damped

Inertial lerping on the scrubbing so video follows the hand without jitter.

Design Decisions

Accessible hotspots

Interface hotspots stay keyboard-reachable and respect reduced motion — spectacle that excludes nobody.

Outcome

✦ Seven of eight planned sections complete

✦ A functional footer with working hotspots

Reflection

“Product storytelling is sequencing: show it, let it land, and only then explain it.”
