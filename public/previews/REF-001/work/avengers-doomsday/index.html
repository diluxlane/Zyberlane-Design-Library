Avengers: Doomsday — Scroll-Driven Cinema

A Marvel-inspired cinematic web experience where scroll conducts everything — video, 3D and story across six choreographed sections.

RoleDesign & Engineering — solo
TimelineJuly 2026
FocusThree.js · GSAP · Creative Dev
SourceGitHub ↗

Context

A scroll-driven cinematic experience inspired by Marvel Studios trailers: six choreographed sections, from a lightning-storm opening to an MCU-timeline finale, built as an educational fan concept.

The Problem

Trailer energy on the web usually means autoplay video and hope. The experiment: can scroll position alone conduct the entire film — every video frame, 3D move and story panel — with no traditional navigation at all?

Process

01

Scroll as the timeline

Frame-by-frame video scrubbing synced to scroll position, with all-intra encoding so frame-seeking is instant in both directions.

02

A procedural centrepiece

A 3D Doctor Doom built in React Three Fiber, character cards orbiting the model, and a custom GLSL atmosphere — particles, volumetric fog, lightning.

03

Performance as a feature

A zero-re-render signal architecture keeps React out of the frame loop; scroll drives shader uniforms directly.

Design Decisions

No play buttons, anywhere

The visitor's scroll is the playhead — committing to one input makes the experience instantly legible.

Outcome

✦ 24 stars and 7 forks on GitHub

✦ Six cinematic sections, fully scroll-conducted

Unofficial fan-made concept — not affiliated with Marvel.

Reflection

“Constraint experiments like this are my interaction-design gym — product work is where that discipline gets spent.”
