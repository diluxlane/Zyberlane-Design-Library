Case Study · 2025

Portfolio-Wide Web Performance

35% organic growth across a client portfolio — design decisions driven by measurement.

RoleSEO & Web Performance
TimelineJan – Apr 2025 · Site Web & Co, Montpellier
FocusCRO · Analytics · Web
+35%

Context

A digital agency's portfolio of B2B and B2C client sites, audited and optimised with Google Analytics, Search Console and keyword research.

The Problem

Beautiful sites nobody found, templates that leaked traffic — the gap between how pages looked and how they performed was invisible to their owners.

Process

01

Audit what actually ranks

Web-performance and SEO audits across the portfolio; the highest-traffic templates got their meta architecture and internal-linking logic rebuilt first.

02

Fix at the template level

One template fix propagates to hundreds of pages — leverage beats page-by-page perfectionism.

03

Report so clients act

Monthly dashboards translated analytics into next actions, not charts.

Design Decisions

Technical SEO before content SEO

Content can't rescue a template that search engines struggle to parse; foundations first.

Outcome

✦ 35% organic-traffic growth across the managed portfolio

Reflection

“Growth work taught me the habit I bring to every design: ship, measure, and let the numbers argue.”
